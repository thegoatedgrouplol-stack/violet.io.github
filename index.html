Engine Capabilities

Local VPN Loopback

Setup Guide

3-Step Deployment

STEP_01

Initial Computer pairing

Install the VioletStore helper tool on macOS or Windows. Connect your iPhone via USB once to sign the initial profile.

STEP_02

Enable WireGuard VPN

Pair the internal WireGuard local tunnel file inside the iOS Settings menu to enable local loopback authentication.

STEP_03

Disconnect PC forever

You are finished! Apps will now auto-refresh background certificates silently whenever you are connected to home Wi-Fi.

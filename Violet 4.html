Get Started

Set up Violet once with a computer. After that, all you need is Wi-Fi.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS/iPadOS 15 or later
  • A Windows, macOS or Linux computer, for the first install only
  • A USB cable
  • An Apple ID
  • A Wi-Fi connection

Install

  1. Install the VPN helper

    On your device, install LocalDevVPN from the App Store and open it. Violet uses it to install and refresh apps over Wi-Fi.

  2. Download the installer

    On your computer, download and run the installer, then connect your device with a USB cable.

  3. Allow the connection

    Tap Allow on your device, then enter the code it shows into the installer and submit it.

  4. Install Violet

    Select your device in the installer, choose Violet (Stable), and wait for it to finish.

  5. Trust the app

    On your device, open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID profile.

  6. Sign in and refresh

    Open Violet, sign in with your Apple ID, then tap the days counter next to Violet in My Apps. If asked to revoke or create a certificate, tap Yes.

Add your first app

  1. Tap +

    In My Apps, tap the plus button at the top left.

  2. Choose an .ipa

    Pick a file from your device, or browse a verified source, and wait for it to appear.

Keep LocalDevVPN on. It must be running whenever you install, update or refresh apps. If it's off, background refresh stops working.
